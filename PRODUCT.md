## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are Sri Lankan brand managers and marketing teams seeking campaign content and spatial brand design for their organizations.

## Product Purpose

Genius arT helps brands turn business objectives into compelling brand experiences across digital, traditional, and physical environments. The website presents the studio's capabilities and portfolio and gives prospective clients a direct path to inquire by phone or WhatsApp.

Success means helping a prospective client understand the studio's offer, see relevant work, and start a project conversation.

## Positioning

Genius arT differentiates itself through the use of AI technology in its creative and production work. It combines strategy and creativity with AI-enabled cinematic content and 3D spatial design in one studio.

## Operating Context

Client work follows a Discover, Develop, and Produce process: understand the business objective, audience, and market; develop and refine creative directions; then produce the selected work.

The studio serves brand activity across digital platforms, traditional media, out-of-home placements, cinemas, conferences, exhibitions, shops, and other physical brand environments.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed services:

- AI-driven brand content
- Cinematic brand storytelling
- 3D design for exhibition stalls
- 3D design for shop branding

The site is a public-facing English-language marketing website. Its primary conversion paths are calling or contacting Genius arT through WhatsApp at +94 77 68 66 068.

Claims about guaranteed outcomes, performance uplift, audience metrics, or other quantified results are not confirmed and must not be presented as evidence without approval.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the client objective, audience, and market context before choosing a creative solution.
- Use AI technology as a meaningful production capability, not as an unsupported outcome claim.
- Unite strategic thinking, creative development, and production in a clear end-to-end process.
- Demonstrate capability through approved real work and accurate project context.
- Make it simple for prospective clients to move from exploration to a direct conversation.
